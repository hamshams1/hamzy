Our Story

From a two-desk trading room to a full-service firm

Sterling Meridian was founded in 1998 by Eleanor Vance and Marcus Okafor, two institutional traders who believed retail and business investors deserved the same quality of execution, research and candor that Wall Street reserved for its largest clients.

Twenty-five years later, we administer over $4.2 billion in client assets for 38,000 individuals, families and companies — still independently owned, still answering our own phones, and still structured so our advisors are paid to be right for you, not to sell you.

1998

The firm is founded

Vance and Okafor open a two-desk brokerage in lower Manhattan focused on honest execution for individual investors.

2004

Advisory practice launched

Registered investment adviser arm established, introducing fee-only fiduciary planning alongside brokerage services.

2011

Corporate desk opens

Treasury management and capital markets services extended to small and mid-sized businesses.

2017

Global market access

Trading connectivity expanded to 40+ exchanges across North America, Europe and Asia-Pacific.

Today

38,000 clients and counting

$4.2B under administration, a 94% client retention rate, and the same founding principle: the client's interest comes first.

Regulatory amp; Compliance

Regulated, examined and accountable

  • Registered broker-dealer with the SEC and member of FINRA. CRD and registration history available through FINRA BrokerCheck.
  • Registered investment adviser subject to fiduciary duty under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940. Form ADV Part 2 available on request.
  • SIPC member. Client securities and cash claims protected up to $500,000 (including $250,000 for cash claims).
  • AML/KYC program administered under the Bank Secrecy Act, with independent annual testing and a designated compliance officer.
  • Net capital and segregation rules enforced above regulatory minimums; client assets never commingled with firm capital.
