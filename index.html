Why Sterling Meridian

A fiduciary standard, not a sales culture

We are structured so that our advisors' interests stay aligned with yours. No payment for order flow on advisory accounts, no proprietary product quotas, and fee schedules published in plain language.

  • Transparent pricing. All commissions, spreads and fees disclosed before you trade — no hidden markups.
  • Independent research. Our analysts hold no positions in the securities they cover.
  • Segregated client assets. Your holdings are held with qualified third-party custodians, never on our balance sheet.
  • Real people, fast answers. Average call answer time under 30 seconds with dedicated relationship managers for managed accounts.

FAQ

Questions investors ask us

Self-directed brokerage accounts have no minimum. Managed portfolio accounts start at $50,000, and corporate treasury mandates are scoped individually. Your advisor will confirm the structure that fits your situation before any agreement is signed.

Brokerage accounts pay per-trade commissions and applicable regulatory fees, published in our fee schedule. Advisory and managed accounts pay an asset-based management fee. We disclose every fee in writing before you fund an account, and we do not accept payment for order flow on advisory accounts.

Client assets are held in segregated accounts with qualified third-party custodians and are never commingled with firm capital. Accounts are protected by SIPC up to applicable limits, and our platform uses bank-grade encryption, two-factor authentication and continuous monitoring. Note that SIPC protection covers custody failure — it does not protect against market losses.
